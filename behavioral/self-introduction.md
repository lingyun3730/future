# Self-Introduction / 自我介绍

面试开场用。先写完所有英文（1 分钟版和 30 秒版），再写所有中文。只使用简历和已确认的事实，不夸大。

# Part 1: English

## 1-Minute Version（约 150 词）

Hi, I'm Lintao. Thanks for taking the time to talk with me.

I'm a backend engineer with over seven years of experience in Payments at eBay.

I've worked across most of the payment system: the Payment Graph that handles transactions at checkout, the gateway that sends them to external processors like Adyen, Checkout.com, and Alipay, the data gateway that processes webhooks and reports, and the accounting system, which books every transaction as journals and is the source of truth for accounting.

In recent years, I've focused on the FX platform. I led several FX projects from requirements to production, working closely with product managers and business partners. One of them extended Buyer FX to 42 additional countries and regions.

I also build internal tools that make daily operations easier, and I use AI tools like Claude Code and GitHub Copilot in my engineering workflow.

Outside work, I go to the gym and enjoy playing table tennis with friends and colleagues.

## 30-Second Version（约 75 词）

Hi, I'm Lintao, a backend engineer with over seven years of experience in Payments at eBay.

I've worked across the payment system, from transaction processing and payment gateways to accounting. Recently, I've focused on the FX platform and led several FX projects end to end, including expanding Buyer FX to 42 additional countries and regions.

I'm excited to talk about how my experience fits this role.

我参与过从交易处理、支付网关到记账的整个支付系统。最近我专注于 FX 平台，端到端主导了多个 FX 项目，其中包括把 Buyer FX 扩展到 42 个新增的国家和地区。

# 使用提示

- 1 分钟版按 "我是谁 → 系统广度 → FX 主导项目 → 工具与 AI → 个人兴趣" 的顺序讲；时间紧张时，只讲 FX 那一段和结尾。
- 30 秒版结尾是引向岗位的过渡句；若不知道岗位细节，可保留这句，不要现编对公司的看法。
- "Checkout.com" 若指的不是支付处理商，请改回原来的 "Checkout"。
- "internal tools" 可按面试岗位改成 EasyFin 等具体项目；没有把握的内容不要放进开场。
- 不要说 "FX earns a lot of money for eBay" 这类没有数据支撑的话；用 42 个国家和地区这类确认过的事实。
- 追问方向：Buyer FX、FXCenter 缓存、EasyFin，分别对应 `../project-coding-examples/` 下的 `buyer-fx-story.md`、`fxcenter-story.md`、`easyfin-story.md`。
